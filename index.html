Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services

How long does editing usually take?

Turnaround time depends on the project length and complexity. Typically, we deliver standard projects within 3-5 business days. Rush services are available for expedited delivery.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept all major video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and more. We can also work with footage from any camera or device. Raw footage and high-quality sources produce the best results.

Do you provide music and sound effects?

Yes! We have access to extensive royalty-free music and sound effect libraries. We can license music on your behalf or work with music you already own.

Can you add subtitles and captions?

Absolutely. We provide professional subtitles and captions in any language. Whether for accessibility or engagement, we can style them to match your content perfectly.

What about revisions and changes?

All our packages include revision rounds. We work with you until you're completely satisfied. Most clients only need 1-2 revision rounds due to our thorough initial consultation.

How do we work together?

We communicate via email, Discord, or video calls. You can share files through Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer. We keep you updated throughout the entire editing process.
