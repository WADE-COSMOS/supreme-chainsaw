Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about joining our team

Do I need professional experience to apply?

No! We welcome editors at all levels. If you're passionate and can demonstrate your skills through your portfolio, we'd love to hear from you.

Can I work part-time or freelance?

Absolutely! We offer flexible work arrangements. You can choose to work full-time, part-time, or take projects as they come.

How much do editors earn?

Rates vary based on experience, project complexity, and specialization. We pay competitive rates that reflect your skill level and the project scope.

What software do I need?

We work with editors using Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, and other professional editing software. You don't need all of them, just proficiency in at least one.

How long is the hiring process?

Typically 2-3 weeks from application to onboarding, depending on availability and project needs.

Do you provide feedback after application?

Yes! We review every application and will provide feedback if we can't move forward, along with suggestions for improvement.
